### 🔬 About Me & Engineering Focus
I am currently pursuing my Master's degree in Mechatronics Engineering at BGU, following my B.Sc. from the Technion. My work focuses on the synergy between **autonomous robotics, control systems, and applied AI/ML**:

### 🚀 Flagship Projects

* **[Resilient I-Bug Navigation](https://github.com/Tsurielvizel/resilient-ibug-spoofing)**  
  Minimalist intensity-based bug navigation under intermittent signal spoofing. Introduces a kinematics-based temporal anomaly filter based on radial Lipschitz bounds. Empirically validated across 1,000,000 paired simulation runs in Julia. *(Supervised by Dr. Andrey Zhitnikov, Technion)*.

* **[NLP Benchmarks for Complex Texts](https://github.com/Tsurielvizel/NLP_Benchs_for_LLM)**  
  Automated LLM-as-a-Judge benchmarking framework evaluating leading LLMs (Claude 3.5/3.7, Gemini 3 Pro) on complex, multi-tiered classical legal and traditional texts. Quantifies category and chain-of-transmission precision and recall.

* **[Whiteboard Lecture Extraction](https://github.com/Tsurielvizel/optimal-control-whiteboard)**  
  End-to-end computer vision pipeline (YOLOv8-seg, OpenCV, temporal motion blur filtering) to automatically extract, restore, and summarize whiteboard lectures for Optimal Control courses.

* **[Alex Kidd FPGA Arcade Recreation](https://github.com/Tsurielvizel/Alex-Kidd)**  
  Hardware implementation and recreation of the classic arcade game on an FPGA using Quartus and HDL *(Technion EE Lab Project)*.

* **[DeepVocalize](https://github.com/Tsurielvizel/DeepVocalize)**  
  End-to-end deep learning pipeline for Hebrew OCR and diacritization using CRNN (ResNet + BiLSTM) and BiLSTM sequence modeling.
